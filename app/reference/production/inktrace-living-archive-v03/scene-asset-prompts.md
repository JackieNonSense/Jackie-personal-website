# Living Archive — independent environment layers

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool, 2026-09-16. Existing approved concept boards are composition references; the approved Wiki workshop is the palette/material reference. No people or readable UI are baked into these layers. Actors, scrolls, cards, paths and text are composited at runtime.
