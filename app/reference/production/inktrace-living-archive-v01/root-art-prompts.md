## Timeline

The reference is STYLE ONLY: warm ivory paper, beige archives, ink charcoal shadows, rare cobalt blue; intricate crafted retro pixel illustration, NOT photorealism, NOT modern SaaS, NOT generic cards. Do not replicate its big open book or any baked-in repeated scene. Build a genuinely different playable miniature setting. Landscape concept board, two equally large panels SIDE BY SIDE showing the SAME scene before and after its functional interaction. Main art stage is top 65% of each panel. A structurally separate RESERVED LOWER READING SLOT occupies next 23%, and narrow stable controls occupy bottom 12%. Absolutely no floating right-hand drawer, no text over people, paths, or clickable targets. Every character stays in stage, every explanation in reading slot. Warm limited palette ivory #EEE8D8, gray #C9C3B7, shadow #57564F, blue #244CB3, ink #23241F. A cobalt slender title bar, small crisp pixel lettering; large detailed pixel art with architected shadows and texture, avoid microscopic UI. The outer border may be charcoal paper, not an entire website. Both states must have equal fixed dimensions. Short carefully placed labels only.
